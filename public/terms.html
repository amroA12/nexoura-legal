Using Nexoura

Nexoura is a learning app for Android. You choose a topic, and Nexoura builds a course of short lessons, quizzes with explanations, and reviews of what you learned. These terms apply when you create an account or use the app. Our privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Who can use Nexoura

You must be at least 13 years old. When you sign up we ask your age range; accounts under 13 are not created. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you may use Nexoura only with the involvement of a parent or guardian who agrees to these terms.

2. Your account

Keep your sign-in details private and tell us if you think someone else used your account. You are responsible for activity on your account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings) or at Delete account.

3. AI-generated content

Lessons, quizzes, explanations and course outlines are written by AI (Google's Gemini). They can contain mistakes. Nexoura is for self-study: it is not professional, medical, legal or financial advice, and a course completion card is not an accredited certificate. Check anything important with a trusted source. You can report a lesson, a question or an explanation in the app; we review reports and remove content that should not be there.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use Nexoura to request or create content that is illegal, hateful, sexual, violent toward others, or that harms children. Do not try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the service, get around its limits, or access other people's accounts. Topics that suggest a risk of self-harm are answered with crisis resources instead of a course. We may refuse a topic, limit generation, or suspend an account that breaks these rules.

5. Free use and subscriptions

You can use Nexoura for free with a set number of new lessons each day and each 30 days, shown in the app. A subscription gives more new lessons each day and each 30 days. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play at the price shown before you buy, renew automatically until cancelled, and are managed and cancelled in Google Play. Refunds follow Google Play's policies. Explanations after each answer and review stay free. We may change plans or prices; a price change applies from your next renewal and Google Play tells you about it first.

6. Your content

The topics you enter, your answers and the feedback you send stay yours. You allow us to use them to run Nexoura for you, to keep it safe, and to improve it, as the privacy policy describes. Do not include personal or sensitive information in topics or feedback.

7. Our content

The app, its design and its brand belong to Nexoura. You may use the lessons generated for you for your own learning. Do not resell or republish the app or its content as a service.

8. Availability and changes

We work to keep Nexoura available, but it may sometimes be unavailable, and features may change. Lessons need a connection to be generated. If we discontinue the service, we will tell you in the app in advance where we can.

9. Disclaimers and liability

Nexoura is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for decisions made on the basis of AI-generated content. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer law that cannot be waived.

If something goes wrong, write to us first at support@getnexoura.com; most problems are solved that way. These terms do not take away the right to bring a claim where the law of your place of residence allows it.

10. Ending your use

You can stop using Nexoura and delete your account at any time. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms; where reasonable, we will tell you why first. Cancel any subscription in Google Play: deleting the account does not cancel it.

11. Changes to these terms

We update the date on this page when the terms change and tell you about important changes in the app before they apply.

12. Contact

For questions about these terms, write to support@getnexoura.com.
