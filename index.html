Privacy Policy

Nexoura

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Nexoura ("we", "our", "the app") is a learning app for Android that helps you build a personalized study path, generated with the help of AI, and track your progress. This page explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

Account informationYour name and email address, when you register. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from your Google account, plus a Google-issued identifier used to recognize your account on later visits — we never see your Google password.
PasswordOnly if you register with email and password. It is stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — we cannot read it, and never store or transmit it in plain text. Accounts created via Google Sign-In have no password at all.
Learning contentThe topics you choose to study, your lesson and quiz progress, quiz answers, and related activity (level, streak, coins, XP) — used to run and personalize the app for you.
Subscription statusIf you subscribe to Nexoura Premium, we verify your purchase with Google Play and store your subscription status. We never see or store your payment card details — Google Play handles payment directly.

How we use this information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • To generate personalized lessons and quizzes for the topics you choose.
  • To track and show you your own learning progress.
  • To verify and grant Premium access if you subscribe.
  • To keep the service secure and diagnose problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Who we share information with

Google Gemini (Google AI)When you study a topic, the topic and your related progress are sent to Google's Gemini AI to generate that lesson's or quiz's content. Google's own privacy policy governs how they process this.
Google PlayUsed for Google Sign-In (authenticating who you are) and for verifying Nexoura Premium subscriptions.

We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party beyond what is listed above.

How we protect your data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS); the app refuses to send data over an unencrypted connection.
  • Your email address is encrypted at rest in our database, not stored as plain text.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way cryptographic hashes.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account, inside the app, at any time. This removes your personal data from our active systems. Encrypted backups kept for disaster recovery are retained for a limited period on a rolling schedule and are then automatically discarded.
  • Contact us: to ask what data we hold about you, or to request a correction, email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Nexoura is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
